Why Germany is such a strong fit

This route is superb for travelers who want rich culture, easy logistics, and a sense of discovering both grand cities and romantic landscapes.

Best known for

Castles and heritage

From fairy-tale palaces to WWII memorials and centuries of architecture, Germany offers depth beyond the obvious highlights.

Great for

Short Europe breaks

It is efficient, scenic, and well-suited to travelers who want a polished itinerary without too much transit.

Add on

Rhine or Bavaria

Build in vineyard towns, lakeside stays, or castle-viewing days for a more atmospheric finish.

Suggested flow

  • Day 1: Arrive in Berlin and settle into a central hotel.
  • Days 2–3: Explore Berlin’s museums, streets, and neighborhoods.
  • Days 4–5: Travel to Munich and the Bavarian countryside.
  • Day 6: Add a castle or lakeside day before departure.

What makes it memorable

  • Effortless rail planning and comfortable transfers
  • Historic cities and postcard-worthy landscapes
  • Excellent for culture, food, and short scenic escapes
