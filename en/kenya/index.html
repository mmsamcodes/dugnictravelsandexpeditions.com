Why Kenya remains iconic

Kenya perfected the safari experience and continues to deliver world-class wildlife viewing with warm hospitality and excellent logistical support.

Best known for

The Great Migration

Witness millions of wildebeest and zebras crossing river crossings in one of nature’s most dramatic spectacles.

Perfect for

Wildlife lovers

From first-time safari-goers to repeat visitors, Kenya offers flexible itineraries and a range of lodge styles.

Add on

Beach time or Rift Valley

Extend your trip with coastal relaxation, Lake Nakuru flamingos, or Hell’s Gate hiking.

Suggested flow

  • Day 1: Arrive in Nairobi and settle into a boutique city hotel.
  • Days 2–4: Safari through Amboseli, the Maasai Mara, and prime game-viewing parks.
  • Days 5–6: Fly to the coast for beach resorts and water activities.
  • Day 7: Depart with unforgettable memories.

What makes it special

  • Expert guides with decades of wildlife knowledge
  • Flexible pacing and accommodation choices
  • Seamless transitions between safari and beach stays
