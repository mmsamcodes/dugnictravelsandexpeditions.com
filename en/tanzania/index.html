Why travelers fall for this route

This is the classic East Africa experience, but curated with comfort, flexibility, and the feeling of discovering something remarkable at every turn.

Best known for

The Great Migration

Witness the drama of the plains and the incredible wildlife density that makes Tanzania one of the world’s most iconic safari destinations.

Perfect for

Couples and families

Luxury tented camps, private guides, and a balanced pace make this a great fit for milestone travels and family adventures.

Worth adding

Island time

Pair the safari with Zanzibar for beach clubs, dhow sails, and a slower final chapter by the sea.

Suggested flow

  • Day 1: Arrive in Arusha and settle into a boutique lodge.
  • Days 2–4: Safari through Tarangire, the Serengeti, and the Ngorongoro Highlands.
  • Days 5–6: Fly to Zanzibar for Stone Town, spice farms, and beach stays.
  • Day 7: Enjoy one final sunrise swim before departure.

What makes it feel special

  • Hot-air balloon safari options at sunrise
  • Private guides and smooth domestic transfers
  • Elegant lodges that balance adventure with comfort
