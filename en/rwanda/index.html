Why this trip feels so special

Rwanda is ideal for travelers who want a high-touch, conservation-led experience that still feels polished and deeply personal.

Best known for

Mountain gorillas

Few experiences on earth feel as moving as standing in the mist with a family of mountain gorillas in their natural habitat.

Great for

Conservation-minded travelers

This is a memorable choice for those who want purpose, scenery, and comfort in one thoughtful itinerary.

Add on

Nyungwe or Lake Kivu

Extend the journey with rainforest treks, canopy walks, or a few serene nights by the lake.

Suggested flow

  • Day 1: Arrive in Kigali and settle into a stylish city stay.
  • Days 2–3: Trek in Volcanoes National Park and enjoy a cultural village visit.
  • Days 4–5: Continue to Lake Kivu for lakeside relaxation and easy excursions.
  • Day 6: Return to Kigali for departure.

What makes it memorable

  • Small-group access and expert local guides
  • Luxury lodges and thoughtful community experiences
  • Strong balance of adventure, comfort, and purpose
