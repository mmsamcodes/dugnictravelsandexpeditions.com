Why this route stands out

South Africa is perfect for travelers who want variety without sacrificing comfort, with dramatic scenery and polished stays throughout.

Best known for

Cape Town and the Winelands

Spend a few days among sea views, famous restaurants, vineyard estates, and the iconic landscapes of the Cape.

Ideal for

First-time Southern Africa visitors

It is one of the easiest ways to experience both city charm and safari adventure in one carefully paced trip.

Add on

Garden Route or Kruger

Choose a scenic coastal extension or a bush-focused finale depending on your pace and style.

Suggested flow

  • Day 1: Arrive in Cape Town and enjoy a relaxed waterfront evening.
  • Days 2–3: Explore Table Mountain, the Peninsula, and the Winelands.
  • Days 4–5: Continue to a private safari reserve for Big Five sightings.
  • Day 6: Return home with a beautifully layered itinerary.

What makes it memorable

  • Scenic transfers and thoughtful logistics
  • Elegant stays with access to excellent dining
  • Perfect balance of city, coast, and wildlife
